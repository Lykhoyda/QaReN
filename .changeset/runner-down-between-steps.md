When QaReN's own device runner (the iOS XCTest runner or the Android runner) stops answering before a step has sent anything to the app, QaReN now checks it before judging the step. A runner that is down is restarted once, attach-only, beside the same app, and the step starts again without the restart counting against its time; the ledger records a `retry` row saying QaReN restarted its own runner. A runner that does not come back refuses the run with `RN_FAST_RUNNER_DOWN` (iOS) or `RN_ANDROID_RUNNER_DOWN` (Android), names QaReN's runner rather than the app or the plan, and gives the next step. At most two restarts are made or adopted per run. For walk-time runner-down refusals, the receipt's next action lists the next rungs and says which ones need the user's OK, while open-time runner-start refusals keep their existing guidance. A restart beside the same app process is recorded and is not an app-continuity interruption; a different or missing app process is reported as the app restarting. A step that has already sent anything, including a tap a recovery, dev-overlay dismissal or login replay made for it, is never sent again. A press whose pixel pre-check could not read the screen is no longer reported as `TARGET_MOVED_BEFORE_DISPATCH` when the runner was down.
