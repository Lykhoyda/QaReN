On Android, a start that has died or has asked Metro for no bundle is now restarted once, or refused at the failed wait that found it instead of at the end of the walk budget. While no wait has returned a debugger target, a failed wait the admission would retry checks Metro's bundle request count (the existing rule, from the attach budget on); when the count does not show the start silent, the first-views gate is not holding the start and at least 41.5 s of the walk budget are left, it also probes the app's process with `adb shell pidof`, cut at 11 s.

QaReN restarts the start once when it launched the app itself with its dev-client link and the walk budget still holds the restart's command allowances (94 s) and the restarted start's own budget (the attach budget, at least 60 s); just before the force-stop it checks again that the stop, its proof and the start (81 s) still leave that budget, and otherwise stops nothing. It first reads the device's process table: a row matching another automation driver refuses as `app_restart_blocked_by_driver` (`wait_for_other_driver`), and a table or process it cannot read as `app_restart_blocked_unreadable` (`check_device_connection`). A start that has moved on meanwhile is left alone. The restart is `am force-stop`, a `pidof` that proves the process gone, and the same `am start`, sent once and never repeated; a failure from the force-stop on is `app_restart_failed` (`check_device_connection`), and the app may be left stopped.

The restarted start is judged again only after its own budget; still dead or silent, it is `app_process_dead_after_restart` (`inspect_app_crash`) or `bundle_not_requested_after_restart` (`check_metro_reachable`). Without the launch link, or with too little of the walk budget left, a dead or silent start is refused at once as `app_process_dead` or `bundle_not_requested`. Each receipt carries the recovery ladder, and the admission facts gain `relaunchBlocked`. iOS is unchanged.
